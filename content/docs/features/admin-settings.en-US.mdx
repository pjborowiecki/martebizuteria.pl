Settings is where an admin manages their own account, the shop's details and its delivery. It is the **Settings** item at the bottom of the sidebar, and it has four header tabs: **My account**, **Sign-in & security**, **Store details** and **Delivery**. The first two act on the signed-in admin's own account, through the same use cases and Better Auth calls as the customer's `/account/profile` page, so they add no permission, table or server function. **Store details** and **Delivery** change the whole shop: saving the seller's details, listing and changing the delivery methods and saving the free delivery threshold require `settings: ["manage"]`, the permission the dashboard and the audit log use, while the seller's details and the threshold themselves are read through the public `getSellerDetails` and `getFreeDelivery` that the storefront and the checkout use.

## Routes

| Route                      | File                          | Shows                                                                               |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/admin/settings`          | `admin.settings.tsx`          | The layout: an `Outlet` for the tab, with the settings copy namespaces              |
| `/admin/settings/`         | `admin.settings.index.tsx`    | Redirects to `/admin/settings/account` in `beforeLoad`                              |
| `/admin/settings/account`  | `admin.settings.account.tsx`  | **My account**: Profile and Preferences                                             |
| `/admin/settings/security` | `admin.settings.security.tsx` | **Sign-in & security**: password, two-factor, sign-in methods, sessions and history |
| `/admin/settings/store`    | `admin.settings.store.tsx`    | **Store details**: the seller's details                                             |
| `/admin/settings/delivery` | `admin.settings.delivery.tsx` | **Delivery**: delivery methods and free delivery                                    |

The paths are `ROUTES.ADMIN_SETTINGS`, `ROUTES.ADMIN_SETTINGS_ACCOUNT`, `ROUTES.ADMIN_SETTINGS_SECURITY`, `ROUTES.ADMIN_SETTINGS_STORE` and `ROUTES.ADMIN_SETTINGS_DELIVERY` in `src/routes.ts`. All of them sit inside the `/admin` layout, so `requireAdmin` guards them and an admin without two-factor authentication is sent to `/admin/security` first (see [Admin dashboard](/docs/features/admin-dashboard#layout)).

The layout declares `staticData.namespaces: ["pages.admin.settings", "pages.account", "pages.auth.errors", "pages.auth.validations"]`. `pages.admin.settings` holds the page's own copy; the other three are there because Settings reuses the account dialogs, which read their copy and validation messages from them.

The account loader prefetches `getCustomerProfileQuery()`. The security loader prefetches it together with `listCustomerSessionsQuery()` and `listCustomerLoginHistoryQuery()`, the store details loader prefetches `getAdminSellerDetailsQuery()`, and the delivery loader prefetches `listAdminDeliveryMethodsQuery()` and `getAdminFreeDeliveryQuery()`, so every tab renders without a loading state.

## Layout

`SettingsPageLayout` (`src/presentation/components/custom/pages/admin/settings/settings-page-layout.tsx`) renders the admin header with the title, description and `SettingsTabs`, which are `AdminHeaderTabLink`s marked active by the route. The body is a single column, at most `max-w-3xl` wide, of `SettingsCard`s. A card has a title, a description, an optional action on the right and, below a divider, its rows; `SettingsRow` is one labelled row with an optional value and action.

The sidebar marks **Settings** active on every `/admin/settings/*` page.

## My account

### Profile

`SettingsProfileCard` reads the profile from `getCustomerProfileQuery()` and shows four rows:

| Row             | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Full name       | **Edit** turns the row into a field. **Save** calls Better Auth's `authClient.updateUser({ name })`                                                                                                  |
| Email address   | **Change** opens `ChangeEmailDialog`. Better Auth sends a confirmation link to the current address, and the address changes once it is opened. A **Not confirmed** badge marks an unverified address |
| Phone number    | **Edit**, as for the name, saved with `updateCustomerPhone`. An empty value clears the number; **Not given** is shown while there is none                                                            |
| Account created | The account's `createdAt`, in the admin's format locale and time zone                                                                                                                                |

`SettingsEditableRow` validates the field with the same `customerAccountZodSchemas.profileForm` the customer profile uses before it saves, and shows the error under the field. `useSaveProfileField` makes the call, toasts **Profile saved.** or an error, and on success invalidates `CUSTOMER_ACCOUNT_QUERY_KEYS.PROFILE` and `SESSION_QUERY_KEYS.CURRENT`, because the cached session holds the name too. A confirmed change of email address is recorded as `auth.email_changed` in [the audit log](/docs/features/audit-log#account-security-events).

### Preferences

`SettingsPreferencesCard` has three fields:

- **Admin panel language.** The list holds `I18N.SUPPORTED_LOCALES`. Choosing the other language reloads the same page with `localizePathname`, keeping the query string: Polish is the default locale and has no prefix (`/admin/settings/account`), English has `/en-US`. The locale middleware then sets the locale cookie to the language in the URL, so the choice holds for the next visit. Choosing the language already shown does nothing.
- **Time zone** and **Date and number format.** These are the same `DisplayPreferenceField`s as on the customer profile. Each list opens with Automatic, labelled with what was detected from the browser, and `updateDisplayPreference` saves the choice to `user.timezone` or `user.format_locale`. Every date, time and number in the admin panel follows them: the order and customer grids, the dashboard's days and months, the audit log and the charts. See [Internationalization](/docs/features/internationalization).

## Sign-in & security

The security route reads the profile once and passes `hasPassword`, `hasGoogle` and `twoFactorEnabled` to the cards as props.

### Password

`SettingsPasswordCard` offers **Change password** for an account with a password, which opens `ChangePasswordDialog`. An account created with Google has none, so the card offers **Set password** instead and opens `SetPasswordDialog`, which calls `setAccountPassword`. That use case refuses a session older than 24 hours, so a stolen older session cannot add a lasting password. Both are described in [Account](/docs/features/account#profile) and [Better Auth](/docs/integrations/better-auth#two-factor-authentication-for-admins).

### Two-factor authentication

`SettingsTwoFactorCard` shows **On** or **Off** and a **Turn on** or **Turn off** button, which opens `TwoFactorDialog`: the password, then the QR code or setup key, a six-digit code and the recovery codes when turning it on, and the password when turning it off. When the dialog finishes, the card invalidates the profile, the session and `CUSTOMER_ACCOUNT_QUERY_KEYS.SESSIONS`, because Better Auth replaces the current session when two-factor authentication changes.

Two notes can appear under the card:

- Where `isAdminTwoFactorRequired()` holds (production and the E2E build), the card says that every admin needs two-factor authentication and that turning it off closes the admin panel until it is turned on again. The next navigation into `/admin` then sends the admin to `/admin/security`.
- On an account without a password, the button is disabled and the card asks for a password first, because turning two-factor authentication on asks for it.

### Sign-in methods

`SettingsSignInMethodsCard` lists **Email and password** and **Google**, each **Connected** or **Not connected**. `getCustomerProfile` reads both from `listAccountProviderIds` (`src/modules/account/account.accessors.ts`), which returns the `provider_id` of every `account` row of the user; `credential` means a password and `google` (`GOOGLE_PROVIDER_ID`) a linked Google account.

When Google is connected, the card warns that signing in with Google does not ask for the shop's six-digit code. Better Auth's `twoFactor` plugin checks the code only on the password sign-in paths, so for a Google sign-in the Google account's own 2-Step Verification takes the place of the shop's code (see [Better Auth](/docs/integrations/better-auth#two-factor-authentication-for-admins)). The owner chose to keep Google sign-in open for admins and to say so here, rather than refuse it.

### Active sessions and sign-in history

`SettingsSessionsCard` lists the signed-in devices with `ActiveSessionList` from the account's sessions page. While any other device is signed in, the card offers **Sign out other devices**, which calls `revokeOtherCustomerSessions`, toasts the result and invalidates `CUSTOMER_ACCOUNT_QUERY_KEYS.SESSIONS`. `SettingsLoginHistoryCard` lists the recent successful and failed sign-ins with `LoginHistoryList`. Both read the same queries as `/account/sessions`.

## Store details

The Store details tab holds one card, **Seller details**, from `src/presentation/components/custom/pages/admin/settings/store/`. It edits the business that runs the shop, which shoppers see in the footer, on the About and product pages, at the checkout, in order e-mails and on the legal pages. The details are columns of the single `store_setting` row (`20261016160000_seller_details.sql`), so changing them needs no release. The migration filled the row with the details the code held until then, so nothing changed for shoppers when it ran.

### Seller details

`SettingsSellerDetailsCard` reads `getAdminSellerDetailsQuery()`, and `SellerDetailsFields` (`seller-details-fields.tsx`) shows these fields, each with its message under it when it is wrong:

| Field                 | What it takes                                                                                                         |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Legal name**        | Up to 200 characters, as in the business register (CEIDG or KRS)                                                      |
| **Street and number** | Up to 120 characters                                                                                                  |
| **Postal code**       | Up to 12 characters, in the `00-000` form when the country is Poland                                                  |
| **City**              | Up to 80 characters                                                                                                   |
| **Country**           | One of the countries in `src/modules/_core/constants/country.ts`, listed by name in the panel's language              |
| **NIP**               | 10 digits with a valid check digit; spaces, dashes and a `PL` prefix are dropped, so the NIP is stored as bare digits |
| **REGON**             | 9 or 14 digits with a valid check digit; spaces and dashes are dropped                                                |
| **Contact email**     | A valid address of up to 254 characters. The hint says that replies to the shop's e-mails go to it too                |
| **Contact phone**     | Up to 30 characters, a valid number written with its country code, such as `+48 600 000 000`                          |

Every field is required. The rules are `storeSettingZodSchemas.sellerDetails` in `src/modules/store-setting/store-setting.zod.ts`. The NIP and REGON checks are `isValidNip` and `isValidRegon` in `src/modules/_core/utils/polish-business-ids.ts`, which the checkout's company invoice NIP uses too, and the phone check is `isValidPhoneNumber` from `libphonenumber-js`. Country names come from `formatCountryName` in `src/modules/_core/utils/country-name.ts`. **Save** runs the same schema in the browser first (`toSellerDetailsSubmission` in `seller-details-form.utils.ts`), so a wrong field is marked before anything is sent, and the server parses the input again.

**Save** calls `updateSellerDetails` (`src/modules/store-setting/use-cases/update-seller-details.ts`), which requires `settings: ["manage"]`, with the details and the time they were last saved as `expectedUpdatedAt`. The use case:

1. reads the stored details and, when nothing changed, returns them without writing, recording or refreshing anything;
2. works out which content pages, and which of their languages, show a changed detail through a store detail placeholder (see [Legal pages: Store details in the pages](/docs/features/legal-pages#store-details-in-the-pages));
3. saves in one D1 batch through `saveSellerDetailsIfUnchanged`: the row is updated only while its `seller_updated_at` still equals `expectedUpdatedAt`, and each of those pages gets the save time as the "Last updated" date of those languages, only once the row carries that time, so a refused save dates nothing;
4. refuses with `CONFLICT` when another admin saved the details after the form was opened, and the card toasts **Someone changed the seller details in the meantime. Reload the page to see the change.**;
5. publishes `scheduleSellerDetailsInvalidation()`, and `scheduleContentPageInvalidation()` when it dated a page, and records `settings.updated` with the target `seller-details` and the changed fields, old and new, in the [audit log](/docs/features/audit-log).

On success the card fills the form with the saved details, refreshes `STORE_SETTING_QUERY_KEYS.ADMIN.SELLER_DETAILS` and `STORE_SETTING_QUERY_KEYS.SELLER_DETAILS`, and toasts **Seller details saved.** The invalidation sends the admin key to the admin hub and the storefront key to the storefront hub, so other admin tabs and open storefront and checkout tabs show the new details without a reload. The account pages and the docs, which do not follow realtime invalidation, show them on their next load.

### Where the details show

The storefront reads the details through the public `getSellerDetails` (`get-seller-details.ts`) and `getSellerDetailsQuery()`, which the `_storefront`, `account`, `docs` and `checkout` layout loaders prefetch, so the first paint already shows them; a failure to read them shows the error page. The admin reads them under its own key through `getAdminSellerDetailsQuery()`, and server code calls `readSellerDetails()`. The country is written out from the stored code in the reader's language.

| Where                                                                        | What it shows                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| The footer and the contact section of `/about`                               | `SellerContactDetails`: the legal name, the address with the country, the NIP and REGON, and the e-mail and phone as links                             |
| Product pages                                                                | The "Manufacturer" section, and the contact e-mail in the invitation to write about another size or re-plating                                         |
| The checkout's pickup step                                                   | `DeliveryInStore`: the street, postal code and city in `deliverySubsteps.inStoreText`                                                                  |
| The receipt on `/withdraw-from-contract` and the account's withdrawal dialog | The contact e-mail                                                                                                                                     |
| The printable order summary                                                  | `OrderSummaryParties`: the legal name, address, country, NIP and e-mail, read under the admin key                                                      |
| Structured data                                                              | `buildStoreStructuredData` on the home page and the manufacturer in `buildProductStructuredData`                                                       |
| E-mails                                                                      | The order confirmation, the thank-you after delivery, the withdrawal acknowledgement and the newsletter's sender footer, and every e-mail's `Reply-To` |
| The legal pages and the FAQ                                                  | Each store detail placeholder in a page's Markdown                                                                                                     |

See [Legal pages](/docs/features/legal-pages#store-details-in-the-pages) and [Transactional email](/docs/features/email#seller-details-in-e-mails) for the last two.

Some places still carry the seller's details outside Settings and need a change of their own: the two PDF forms in `public/forms`, which have the details printed in them (the withdrawal form is attached to every order confirmation) until they are generated from the stored details; the public business details in the Stripe Dashboard; the sender profile of the InPost organisation; and the Google OAuth consent screen. `APP_NAME` in `src/presentation/branding/app.ts`, the brand name the two-factor issuer, the e-mail sender's name and Stripe's `appInfo` use, stays a constant.

## Delivery

The Delivery tab holds two cards, from `src/presentation/components/custom/pages/admin/settings/delivery/`. [Shipping and delivery](/docs/features/shipping-and-delivery#managing-delivery-methods) describes the rules the server applies and what the checkout does with them.

### Delivery methods

`SettingsDeliveryMethodsCard` reads `listAdminDeliveryMethodsQuery()` and lists the methods that are not archived in the order the checkout offers them. Each row shows the type's icon, the name in the panel's language, the type and the carrier (for example **Courier · InPost**), the price or **Free**, a **Hidden** badge for a method the checkout does not offer and **Carrier switched off** for one whose carrier is off in D1. The arrows move a method up or down; the new order is saved at once, the toast says **Checkout order saved.**, and the checkout lists the delivery families in that order.

**Add method** opens `DeliveryMethodDialog` with these fields:

| Field                                   | What it takes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Type**                                | **Courier**, **Parcel locker** or **Pickup in person**                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Carrier**                             | An existing carrier, or **New carrier** with its **Carrier name** (up to 60 characters). A parcel locker always uses InPost, and for a carrier that is not InPost the dialog warns that its parcels are booked on its own site, with the tracking number saved on the order |
| **Name in Polish**, **Name in English** | Both required, up to 80 characters each; shoppers see the name in their language                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Price**                               | In PLN; 0 makes the method free                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Offer at checkout**                   | Off keeps the method in the list, hidden from the checkout                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

**Edit** opens the same dialog for an existing method with its type and carrier under the title, since neither can change, and offers the names, the price and **Offer at checkout**. Its **Archive** button asks for confirmation first: the method disappears from the checkout, and orders already placed with it keep it. Archived methods are listed under **Archived methods** below the list, each with **Restore**, which brings the method back switched off, so it is offered again only once it is turned on.

A refused change is shown as a toast in the panel's language: a second offered parcel locker or pickup method, switching off or archiving the last offered method (the toast points to the sales switch on the dashboard instead), a parcel locker with another carrier, and an edit of a method someone changed in the meantime, which asks to reload the page. `useDeliveryMethodActions` refreshes the Settings list and the checkout's list after every change, and every change reaches other admin tabs, storefront tabs and open checkouts through realtime invalidation.

### Free delivery

`SettingsFreeDeliveryCard` reads `getAdminFreeDeliveryQuery()`. **Offer free delivery** turns the threshold on or off, and **Free delivery from** takes the products' value in PLN from which delivery costs nothing, counted after a discount code and before delivery. **Save** calls `setFreeDeliveryThreshold`, with no value when the switch is off, and toasts **Free delivery saved.**; with the switch on and no value above zero, the field asks for one instead. The checkout then shows delivery as free once a basket reaches the value and tells shoppers how much they have left to spend before that; an open checkout follows a change at once. See [Shipping and delivery: Free delivery](/docs/features/shipping-and-delivery#free-delivery).

Every change on this tab is recorded in the [audit log](/docs/features/audit-log) under the `settings` category.

## Dialog styling

The account dialogs take a `variant`: `"editorial"`, the default, for the customer's account pages, and `"default"` for the admin. Settings passes `variant="default"` to `ChangeEmailDialog`, `ChangePasswordDialog`, `SetPasswordDialog` and `TwoFactorDialog`, so they match the admin panel's other dialogs, and `AccountDialogEyebrow` hides itself in that variant. See [shadcn components](/docs/ui/shadcn-components#dialogs) for the two variants.

## Relation to `/admin/security`

`/admin/security` (`admin_.security.tsx`) stays as the enrolment gate. It is outside the `/admin` layout and guarded by `requireAdminRole` alone, so an admin without two-factor authentication, whom `requireAdmin` keeps out of the panel, can still turn it on there. It is not in the sidebar: once two-factor authentication is on, the admin manages it, the password and the sessions under **Settings → Sign-in & security**.

## Not in Settings yet

Settings covers the admin's own account, the seller's details and the shop's delivery for now. Other shop-wide settings are not in it yet: the carriers themselves, the team of admins, staff notifications, and the payment and integration status. Sales are paused from the [sales switch](/docs/features/admin-dashboard#sales-switch) on the overview, and the legal pages are edited under [Content](/docs/features/legal-pages), where they take the seller's details from Store details through placeholders.
